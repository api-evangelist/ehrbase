EHRbase 2.0 Enhanced Stability, Performance and Compliance. Munich, 09/04/2024 – We are thrilled to announce: The highly anticipated version 2.0 of EHRbase has been officially released today. EHRbase is renowned as the leading open source Electronic Health Record (EHR) backend based on the internationally recognised openEHR specifications.
