Dear openEHR community, Among the objectives of the openEHR Education Program Board (EPB) is to provide strategic direction and leadership for openEHR education and training activities, as well as to develop a framework for examination and certification based on professional skills and roles. The EPB would like to gather your feedback on the community’s primary needs and priorities. To that end, we have created a very short questionnaire (2 minutes) to help us validate our progress so far and define our next steps.
