Hi everyone, I have been working hard on something new for the community, and it is finally at the point where I want to show it to you. It is called FerroTERM, a FHIR terminology server written in Rust. It started because every time I wanted to validate a coded field next to FerroEHR I had to docker compose an big stack with lots of RAM, and at some point that got annoying enough that I wrote my own.
