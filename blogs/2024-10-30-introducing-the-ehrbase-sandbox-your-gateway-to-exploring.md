Introducing the EHRbase Sandbox Your Gateway to Exploring the Power of openEHR We are excited to announce the launch of the EHRbase Sandbox , the simplest and most accessible way to begin your journey with openEHR . The Sandbox connects to a public test server instance of EHRbase , providing a user-friendly environment to explore and experiment with the core functionalities of openEHR. With an intuitive frontend, the Sandbox allows users to: Upload and test their own data definitions (openEHR Templates).
