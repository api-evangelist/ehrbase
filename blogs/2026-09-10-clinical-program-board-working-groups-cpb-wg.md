Hello everyone, I have noticed a lot of posts on Discourse recently that seem to be requesting some aid on work that we have been doing in the background at the Clinical Program Board (CPB) over the past few months. I was planning to make an announcement on social media (Discourse, LinkedIn, etc.) after EHRCON26, but I think it’s a good idea to share this here on Discourse for now. By definition, a Working Group (WG) is a group of people who meet regularly to work on a shared topic and produce something useful for others like guidelines, proposals, documentation, or practical examples.
